# ✈️ Airline Passenger Satisfaction Analysis  

---

## 🎯 Project Overview  
This project presents an in-depth analysis of customer satisfaction in the airline industry using a comprehensive dataset.  
By examining key factors such as **inflight services**, **booking convenience**, and **overall satisfaction ratings**, we aim to uncover insights that guide airlines in enhancing passenger experience and service quality.  

Through **advanced visualization tools** — *Shiny R*, *Tableau*, and *Power BI* — this analysis identifies patterns and relationships within the data, offering actionable recommendations to improve customer satisfaction and foster loyalty.  

---

## 🧩 Key Features  
- **Interactive Dashboards** built using Shiny R, Tableau, and Power BI.  
- **Dynamic Filtering & Comparison** of airlines by age, class, gender, and service type.  
- **Visual Exploration** of satisfaction factors like seat comfort, cleanliness, and flight delays.  
- **Comprehensive Analysis** of demographic and operational trends influencing satisfaction.  
- **Data-driven Recommendations** to enhance service quality and passenger loyalty.  

---

## 🧠 Tools & Technologies  
- **R & Shiny R** – for interactive web-based visualizations.  
- **Tableau** – for comparative analytics and demographic visualizations.  
- **Power BI** – for KPI tracking, airline benchmarking, and dynamic dashboards.  
- **Python & Excel** – for preprocessing and data transformation.  

---

## 🖼 Visualization Highlights  

### 🔹 Shiny R  
- Airline comparison tools with satisfaction metrics.  
- Delay pattern visualizations (arrival vs departure).  
- Gender-based satisfaction distribution analysis.  

### 🔹 Tableau  
- Passenger demographics by age, class, and gender.  
- Delay vs satisfaction level bar charts.  
- Service satisfaction trends by flight distance and class.  

### 🔹 Power BI  
- KPI metrics for inflight service performance over time.  
- Airline satisfaction gauge charts.  
- Flight delay trend analysis by year and airline.

## 🚀 Insights & Recommendations  
- Prioritize **seat comfort, cleanliness, and inflight entertainment** to enhance satisfaction.  
- Address **operational inefficiencies** related to departure and arrival delays.  
- Tailor services based on **demographic trends** (age, gender, class).
